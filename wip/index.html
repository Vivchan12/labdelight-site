Example situation: a 25-person services team wants to use AI on proposals.

01

The problem we agreed on

Proposals take two weeks because pricing sign-off waits on one partner, not because writing is slow.

02

Who it affects

Account leads, the pricing partner, and clients waiting on a quote.

03

What changes, what stays human

AI drafts a first proposal from past wins. Pricing judgement stays with people, with a clear rule for who signs off.

04

Assumptions to test

Past proposals are clean enough to reuse. Partners will accept a delegated sign-off under a set value.

05

Recommendation

Fix the sign-off rule first, with no new software. Then test a drafting prototype with two account leads for two weeks.

06

How we’ll know it worked

Time from request to quote, and whether account leads still use the draft after a month.
